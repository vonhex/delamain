# Station Kepler-Nine Is Silent

## Pitch

Eleven days ago, mining station Kepler-Nine, a spinning ring bolted to a black asteroid at the edge of the Halden Belt, stopped transmitting. Thirty-eight people worked there. You are the crew of the relief tug Halfpenny, four days late and short on fuel, and your docking lights are the only lights on the station. Somewhere inside, something is listening to the sound of your boots.

## DM Truths

1. Kepler-Nine, owned by the Tessaline Extraction Concern, mines iridium from asteroid KN-9. It has a rotating habitat ring (normal gravity), a zero-gravity Hub, and the Shaft, a 900-meter elevator down to the Deep Works and the fusion reactor.
2. Twenty-three days ago, Drill Team Four broke into a sealed geode cavern at depth 840 meters. The cavern was lined with pale violet crystal that hummed. The crystal is alive: a silicate colony the crew nicknamed the Lull, grown slowly in the cold rock for ages.
3. The Lull feeds on heat and electricity. It senses only vibration: footsteps, voices, machinery, heartbeats if you touch metal. It cannot see. It cannot sense anything that is still and silent. Below minus 20 Celsius it goes dormant.
4. The Lull spreads as glassy veins through metal, fastest along power lines. It catches people and slowly encases them in crystal. These pillars stay alive about thirty days, dreaming and humming. A pillar can be freed by cold: chill it below minus 20 and the crystal goes brittle and cracks off (Might DC 13 to break safely).
5. The Lull mimics. It vibrates hull plates and speakers to copy sounds it has heard: knocks, the PA chime, and in the last few days, voices. Its voices are always slightly wrong: it repeats the last words it heard, and it cannot say anything new. That is the test: ask a voice a question it has never heard, and the Lull can only echo.
6. Station Director Augustin Rhee reported the find to Tessaline. Tessaline's reply, signed by Vice President Corinna Sabe, ordered: "Preserve the specimen. Continue operations. Do not evacuate." Rhee obeyed. Eight days later, the Lull reached the reactor.
7. The relief crew's corporate liaison carries sealed orders (Order 7-Sable) to bring back a living sample of the Lull in a cold flask, by force if needed, and leave no witnesses who know about Order 7. A pregenerated hero, Yusra Okafor-Brandt, holds these orders, and gets to choose whether to follow them.
8. Survivors: three people are alive and free. Dr. Wren Achterberg, the geologist, hides in Cold Storage C (minus 25). Tomasz Pietrowski, a 19-year-old mechanic, hides in the Hydroponics bay and has stopped speaking out of fear. Director Rhee is a pillar in the Command deck, but still dreams and murmurs.
9. Of the other 35 crew: 29 are pillars scattered through the station, 6 died in the first week (faded out; found as covered shapes in the Medical bay).
10. The only ways to beat the Lull: freeze the station (open the ring's radiator vents and kill the heaters; everything drops below minus 20 in 3 hours, killing the Lull and freeing the pillars but dangerous to anyone without cold suits); or overload the reactor and destroy the station (kills everything, including the pillars); or leave and quarantine it with a warning beacon.

## Table Notes

Slow dread, not gore. Let silence be the scariest thing. Bodies are faded out: covered shapes, no details. Whenever the heroes speak loudly, run machines or fire weapons, mark the Lull Attention clock. Reward players who whisper in character. Pace: Act 1 (45 min), Act 2 (60), Act 3 (75), Act 4 (45).

Recurring details: the hum, a low note just under hearing that you feel in your teeth. The cold breath fogging inside helmets. The PA chime, two soft tones, which the Lull imitates. Violet light glimmering through glass veins in the walls, brighter near power.

## Characters

- **Captain Marisol Teague-Oduya**, relief tug captain. Might +1, Agility +1, Wits +1, Spirit +3. HP 14. Gear: flare pistol (d8, sets fires, blinds), command codes for Kepler-Nine docking, handheld radio. Hook: her younger brother, Ilan Teague, is a driller on Kepler-Nine. He was on Drill Team Four. He is a pillar in the Deep Works.
- **Bao Lindqvist**, engineer. Might +2, Agility +0, Wits +3, Spirit +0. HP 13. Gear: plasma cutter (d8, cuts doors), toolbelt, portable power cell. Hook: Bao designed half of Kepler-Nine's heating system ten years ago and knows the radiator vents can be opened from the Hub.
- **Dr. Femi Castellanos**, medic. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +2, Spirit +2. HP 11. Gear: med kit (heal d6+2, four uses), sedative injector (puts a human to sleep), sample case with a cold flask. Hook: was Wren Achterberg's university roommate; Wren's last private message to Femi, 12 days ago, said "If they send you, bring a coat."
- **Yusra Okafor-Brandt**, Tessaline corporate liaison. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +2, Spirit +2. HP 12. Gear: compact pistol (d6), company override card (opens any Tessaline door), sealed orders. Hook: Order 7-Sable tells her to bring back a living sample and silence witnesses. She may tell the others, or not. Tessaline holds her mother's medical contract.
- **Grisha Mbeki-Vold**, cargo hand and ex-soldier. Might +3, Agility +1, Wits +0, Spirit +1. HP 16. Gear: rivet gun (d8, loud), crowbar (d6, quiet), magnetic boots. Hook: wants the hazard bonus, 20,000 credits, for a farm on Calloway Moon. Stutters when nervous; very good at staying still.
- **Pip**, the crew's small maintenance drone. Might +0, Agility +3, Wits +1, Spirit +0. HP 6. Gear: camera, small arm, speaker. Hook: for a player who wants an odd role. Pip's wheels vibrate, so the Lull notices it easily.

## Opening Scene

The ring turns slowly, every window black, against an asteroid the color of old coal. Docking clamps catch with a long groan through both hulls. On the other side of the airlock, the station is cold (4 Celsius), air is breathable but stale, and the lights are at emergency red, one strip in three working. Then, from somewhere in the station, the PA chime sounds: two soft tones. A voice says, "Welcome to Kepler-Nine. Welcome to Kepler-Nine." Then silence. Scanners show no life signs, confused by something in the metal itself. Ask each player who they are, then: do they go in together, split up, or call out?

## Spoken Opening

Station Kepler-Nine turns slowly against the dark, a great ring bolted to an asteroid the color of old coal. Every window is black. Your docking lights are the only lights anywhere on it.

The clamps catch. A long groan travels through both hulls and into your bones, and then the Halfpenny is still.

Eleven days ago this station stopped talking. Thirty-eight people worked here. You are four days late.

The airlock cycles open. The air on the other side is breathable, but stale, and cold. Four degrees. Your breath fogs. The corridor lights burn emergency red, one strip in three, and the rest is shadow.

Then, from somewhere deep inside the station, the public address chime sounds. Two soft tones. A calm voice says, "Welcome to Kepler-Nine. Welcome to Kepler-Nine."

Then nothing.

Your scanners show no life signs. The readings flicker and slide, confused, as if something in the metal itself is interfering. Beneath it all there is a hum, a low note just under hearing, that you feel in your teeth.

Do you go in together, split up, or call out?

## Acts

### Act 1: The Quiet Ring

**Goal:** find out what happened and whether anyone is alive.

**Scenes:**
- **Docking Ring.** Frost inside the windows. A sign taped to the inner airlock: "BE QUIET. DON'T RUN. -W." The Halfpenny's collar was scraped while docking; Bao can tell (Wits DC 10) the collar seal is slowly leaking, which starts the Air clock.
- **Mess Hall.** Frozen food on trays, chairs knocked over. A pillar stands by the coffee urn: a woman in coveralls, encased in violet glass, humming. Name tag: Priya Raman, cook. Touching it raises the hum (mark Lull Attention). Femi finds (Wits DC 13) a very slow heartbeat.
- **Command Deck.** Director Augustin Rhee is a pillar in his chair. Leaning close in silence, you hear him murmur: "Sabe said preserve. Preserve. Don't evacuate." The console runs on emergency power; the logs need a code. Rhee's code hint is taped under the desk: "My daughter's age, times my years here." His family photo says "Lena, age 7" and his crew file says he has been on station 6 years. Code: 42. Static noises from the console mark Lull Attention.
- **Director's Logs.** Day 1: Drill Team Four breaches a geode at 840 meters, finds singing crystal. Day 3: Tessaline replies (Sabe: preserve, continue, do not evacuate). Day 6: first crew member, Ilan Teague, encased in the Deep Works. Day 9: "It hears us. It does not see us." Day 12: the reactor. Last entry, Day 12: "Wren says cold. Everything cold. I couldn't make myself do it."
- **Medical Bay.** Six covered shapes (fade out). Medical officer's note: "Pillars alive. Heartbeat 4 per minute. Crystal brittle at low temp; sample in freezer cracked by itself."

**Clues:**
- The Lull hunts by sound and vibration: the sign "BE QUIET", Rhee's Day 9 log, or watching a vein brighten when someone speaks loudly.
- Pillars are alive: Femi's check in the Mess Hall, or the medical note.
- Tessaline knew and ordered no evacuation: Rhee's log, or Rhee's murmuring.
- Cold is the weakness: medical note, or Rhee's last log.

**Ends when:** the crew decides to look for survivors (the sign is signed "W") or tries to leave.

### Act 2: Survivors

**Goal:** find Wren and Tomasz and learn the truth about the Lull.

**Scenes:**
- **The Voice in the Vents.** A voice through a wall grille: "Help me, please, I'm in Hydroponics." It is the Lull, echoing Tomasz's whisper from days ago. Asked anything new, it only repeats itself. Wits DC 13 notices it never breathes.
- **Hydroponics.** Overgrown and warm (grow lamps on their own battery), so violet veins wrap the planters. Tomasz Pietrowski hides on a water tank, perfectly still, and will not speak. Moving through quietly is Agility DC 13 each; a failure clangs and marks Lull Attention twice. He finger-writes on a fogged window: "Wren. Cold Storage C. Knock three, then two."
- **Cold Storage C.** Minus 25, frost everywhere, no veins at all. Knock three then two (the signal Tomasz gave; Wren also scratched "3 + 2" on the door frame, findable by Wits DC 10). Wren Achterberg opens the door in a thermal suit, with frostbitten fingers. She tells them everything in Truths 2 through 5 and 10. She has been tracking it: it spreads one deck per day toward heat. She has a plan but can't do it alone: someone must open the radiator vents from the Hub and someone must cut the heater bus in the Deep Works.
- **The Echo Test.** Wren insists on testing everyone before she trusts them: "Tell me something I never said." If the crew asked the vent voice a new question, they already understand. If anyone tells her about Femi's message ("bring a coat"), Wren breaks down laughing and crying.

**Clues:**
- The Lull echoes but cannot say new words: the vent voice test, or Wren tells them.
- Ilan Teague is a pillar in the Deep Works at 840 meters: Rhee's log, or Wren.
- The freezing plan: Wren, or Bao reading the heating schematics in Command (Wits DC 13).

**Ends when:** the crew commits to a plan: freeze, overload, or flee.

### Act 3: The Shaft

**Goal:** descend to the Deep Works to cut the heater bus (for the freeze) or set the reactor to overload, and maybe rescue Ilan.

**Scenes:**
- **The Elevator.** A cage on a 900-meter cable, four minutes down. The motor marks Lull Attention once, unless Bao runs it quietly off the power cell (Wits DC 13). Halfway down, at 450 meters, the cage shudders: the PA chime sounds, and a voice says "Welcome to Kepler-Nine." The Lull knows they are coming.
- **Deep Works Gallery.** 30 Celsius from the reactor; violet crystal grows over everything like frost in reverse. Eleven pillars stand in the gallery, humming in a chord. One is Ilan Teague. The heater bus panel is at the far end, 60 meters through the pillars. Crossing silently: Agility DC 13 per hero; failure marks Lull Attention.
- **The Geode.** In the glowing cavern stands the Heart, a three-meter crystal mass pulsing slow violet. Weapons cannot hurt it (it heals); cold kills it. Yusra's orders say to take a fist-sized shard in the cold flask here. The Heart reacts to theft by screaming through every hull plate on the station: mark Lull Attention twice.
- **Yusra's Choice.** At the Geode, Yusra's company card pings: a new message from VP Sabe. "Sample is priority. Witnesses are a liability." The player decides. If Yusra reveals the order, the crew can use it later as proof against Tessaline.
- **The Heater Bus or the Reactor.** Cut the heater bus with Bao's cutter (Wits DC 13, loud, 2 marks of Attention). Or set the reactor to overload: 45 minutes to detonation, cannot be stopped once set (Wits DC 16 to set).

**Clues:**
- Ilan can be freed by cold: Wren's knowledge, Medical note.
- Tessaline wants a sample and no witnesses: Yusra's message, or Rhee's Command logs show Sabe's original order.

**Ends when:** the bus is cut, the reactor is set, or the crew retreats up the shaft.

### Act 4: Cold Burn

**Goal:** finish the plan and get off the station alive.

**Scenes:**
- **The Hub.** Zero gravity. The radiator vent controls are behind a frosted panel. Opening the vents takes three steps: unlock (Yusra's card or Bao's Wits DC 13), prime (Wits DC 13), and turn three manual valves (Might DC 13 each, or one hero rolls all three at DC 16). Once open, the station falls to minus 20 in three hours of game time, which the DM can fast-forward.
- **The Lull Wakes.** As the heat drains, the Lull panics and grows fast toward the warmest thing left: the Halfpenny. Veins creep along the docking collar. Crawlers (see Threats) form from loose crystal and come for the noisiest people.
- **Shelter in Cold.** While the station freezes, the crew must hide in Cold Storage C or in their suits, perfectly silent, while the Lull searches (one group Spirit DC 13 to keep still and quiet; Grisha gets +2). A failure means a crawler finds them.
- **Thaw and Waking.** When the cold takes hold, the hum stops. One by one, pillars crack and people fall out, gasping, alive. 29 pillars, 25 survive if the freeze is finished before the Rot clock ends; fewer otherwise.
- **The Collar.** If veins reached the Halfpenny, Bao or Grisha must cut the collar free (Might DC 13) and the tug must vent its own heat to kill the crystal clinging to it.

**Ends when:** the crew leaves, with or without survivors, sample or no sample.

## NPCs

- **Dr. Wren Achterberg**, station geologist. *Voice:* whispers always, fast and precise, counts seconds under her breath, laughs at the worst moments. *Wants:* to freeze the station and save the pillars. *Knows:* everything about the Lull's senses and weakness. *Secret:* she was the one who recommended breaching the geode. *If threatened:* goes completely still and silent, which works on the Lull but unsettles people.
- **Tomasz Pietrowski**, 19, station mechanic. *Voice:* silent; narrate his hand signs and finger writing on fogged glass. *Wants:* to go home to Lublin Station. *Knows:* the knock code, the crawl spaces of the ring. *If threatened:* hides somewhere impossible.
- **Director Augustin Rhee**, pillar. *Voice:* sleepy murmur, repeating "Preserve. Don't evacuate." If freed, a tired man who apologizes for everything. *Secret:* he kept the order to himself and let his crew believe it was safe. *If threatened:* he is encased; afterward he accepts any punishment.
- **Ilan Teague**, Marisol's brother, pillar in the Deep Works. *Voice:* if freed, cheerful, calls his sister "Mari". *Knows:* what the Heart felt like: "It was lonely. It was so cold for so long and then we came in warm."
- **VP Corinna Sabe**, Tessaline executive (only by message). *Voice:* smooth, reasonable, every sentence starts with "Of course." *Wants:* the sample. *If threatened:* lawyers.
- **The Lull.** *Voice:* only echoes, always slightly too slow, with the PA chime before it. Never invents new words.

## Locations

- **Docking Ring.** Frost on windows, Wren's sign, the Halfpenny's scraped collar.
- **Mess Hall.** Frozen trays, the pillar of cook Priya Raman.
- **Command Deck.** Rhee's pillar, console code 42, logs.
- **Medical Bay.** Six covered shapes, the medical note, a freezer with a cracked crystal sample.
- **Hydroponics.** Warm, overgrown, thick with veins; Tomasz on the water tank.
- **Cold Storage C.** Minus 25, no veins, Wren's refuge, knock three then two.
- **The Hub.** Zero gravity, radiator vent controls, three manual valves.
- **The Shaft and Deep Works.** Cage elevator 900 meters, gallery at 840 meters with eleven pillars, heater bus panel, reactor room, the Geode with the Heart.
- **The Halfpenny.** Cramped tug, warm, smells of coffee; the last warm place.

## Threats

- **Crawlers** (1 per Lull Attention step from 2 up, max 6). Knee-high clusters of violet crystal that skitter on metal. HP 6 each. Attack +3, d6 damage, on a hit the target is Crusted (cannot move until someone spends a turn breaking it off, Might DC 10). Special: blind, they hunt sound; a hero who freezes in place and stays silent is ignored. Cold (a fire extinguisher blast, cold flask, or vented room) shatters them instantly.
- **The Heart.** Cannot be fought. If touched, d8 damage and the hero is Crusted. Dies only to cold.
- **Vein Surge.** When Attention hits 4, walls pulse and any hero touching metal takes d6 and must make Spirit DC 13 or freeze in fear for one turn.
- **Yusra's orders.** A human threat if the player chooses it.
- **Ways around:** silence, stillness, cold, decoys (a thrown radio playing noise draws every crawler for 5 minutes; Pip makes an excellent decoy).

## Clocks

- **Air (Halfpenny collar leak):** 1) docked, slow hiss; 2) after Act 2, the tug's reserves at half; 3) during Act 3, the collar must be resealed (Wits DC 13) or the tug cannot hold air for the flight home; 4) unsealed at the end of Act 4, only four people fit in suits for the flight.
- **Power:** 1) emergency red lights; 2) lights fail on the ring, only handlamps; 3) elevator power at 20 percent, one more trip; 4) station dark, heaters dead (which actually helps the freeze).
- **Lull Attention:** 1) humming rises; 2) PA chimes and voices follow the crew, first crawler; 3) crawlers hunt the crew; 4) Vein Surge, all crawlers converge; reset to 2 after the crew hides silently for a scene.
- **Rot (pillars):** 1) day 23 since breach, pillars healthy; 2) after Act 3, the oldest pillars (Ilan and the Drill Team) weaken; 3) if the freeze starts late, 5 pillars will not wake; 4) if the station is not frozen by the end, the pillars do not survive.

## Rewards

- Hazard bonus: 20,000 credits each from Tessaline, paid only if the crew signs a silence agreement.
- Order 7-Sable and Sabe's messages: proof that ends Sabe's career and gets every survivor a 200,000 credit settlement, if the crew goes public.
- A sample of the Lull in the cold flask: worth 2 million credits to Tessaline; dangerous if it warms.
- Survivors' gratitude: Wren, Tomasz, Rhee, Ilan and up to 25 others owe their lives to the crew.
- Wren's insight: the Lull is not evil, only hungry and cold; she can tell any future crew how to live with it.

## Endings

- **Triumph (Frozen Dawn):** the station is frozen in time, up to 25 pillars are freed, the sample is left behind or destroyed, and Yusra's orders go public. The Halfpenny limps home with the survivors packed in like sardines, singing.
- **Bittersweet (The Beacon):** the crew flees or overloads the reactor. They live, Wren and Tomasz with them, but the pillars are lost. Marisol lights a candle for Ilan every year.
- **Disaster (Specimen):** the sample comes home warm. Six months later, Tessaline's research station on Calloway Moon stops transmitting. Grisha's farm was on Calloway Moon.

## Off the Map

- **People:** Chief Driller Halvard Nkemelu, Comms Officer June Iwasaki, Cargo Boss Delphine Ruiz, Pilot Ezra Coldwater (all pillars), Nurse Benedikt Saar (a covered shape). No children were aboard.
- **Places:** Recreation Deck (a cracked aquarium, all fish frozen), the Chapel (a quiet room with 38 candles, 6 lit), Ore Processing (huge, loud machinery, dangerous to start), Storage Bay D (cold suits, 6 sets), the Observation Blister (view of the black rock and distant sun).
- **If they leave the story:** the Halfpenny can simply leave; the Air clock still runs, and Tessaline's next ship, the Concord, arrives in 30 days with orders to harvest the Heart. The Lull, carried home, wakes somewhere warmer.
