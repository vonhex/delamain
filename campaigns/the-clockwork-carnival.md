# The Clockwork Carnival

## Pitch

Every autumn, Madame Ottilie Sprocket's Clockwork Carnival rolls into the town of Puddingford with brass rides that whir and sing. This year the gates opened by themselves at dawn, the carousel horses are galloping around the meadow, the popcorn machine is chasing people, and Madame Sprocket has vanished. The mayor has asked you to find her before the Grand Finale at sunset.

## DM Truths

1. Every attraction in the carnival is powered by the Wishing Mainspring, a golden spring the size of a dinner plate kept in a little locked box in the ringmaster's wagon. Madame Ottilie Sprocket built it 40 years ago. It runs on happy wishes from carnival visitors.
2. Last night, Ottilie decided to retire. She is 76, tired, and out of money. She signed a paper agreeing to sell the whole carnival tonight at sunset to Mr. Barnaby Gristle of Gristle's Gadget Works for 800 gold crowns. He plans to take the attractions apart for spare gears.
3. The attractions overheard. Professor Pendulum, the clockwork fortune-teller, and Clementine, the lead carousel horse, panicked. At midnight they wound the Wishing Mainspring too tight so the attractions would wake up and could save themselves. Now every attraction can move and talk.
4. Then they hid Ottilie, very gently, inside the giant music box at the center of the carnival, called the Grand Calliope, with a pillow, tea and biscuits. They believe that if Ottilie cannot sign the final handshake at sunset, the sale cannot happen. She is safe, cozy and very cross.
5. The Grand Calliope opens only when someone plays the ringmaster's song on its keys: the notes are hidden in Ottilie's catchphrase, "Come one, come all, come see." In the calliope's language, "come" means the low note, "one" is the middle, "all" the high, and "see" the very high. The tune is low, middle, low, high, low, very high, sung or hummed. Players can solve it by speaking or singing.
6. The overwound Mainspring is a problem: by sunset it will snap loose, and every ride will spin faster and faster until they all fall over in a giant heap and stop forever. It must be eased back one turn using the golden Winding Key, which Clementine swallowed for safekeeping.
7. Ottilie does not want to sell. If the carnival can earn 800 gold crowns by sunset, or if Mr. Gristle can be convinced to let the attractions keep their gears, she would gladly keep going or hand the carnival to someone new. Her grand-niece Nettie is the natural heir.
8. Mr. Gristle is not wicked, just grumpy and lonely. As a boy he rode this very carousel; he forgot. If reminded (there is a photograph of young Barnaby on Clementine in Ottilie's wagon), he softens.
9. The carnival has 9 attractions: the Grand Calliope, the Carousel, the Popcorn Engine, the Hall of Mirrors, the Riddle Clown, the Ferris Wheel, the Tin Strongman, the Teacup Spinner and Professor Pendulum's booth.

## Table Notes

This is for kids and grown-ups together. Peril is silly or gentle: things get sticky, dizzy, tangled or very confused. No one is ever hurt badly; at 0 HP a hero is "too dizzy to stand" and sits out until a friend helps them up. Encourage singing, funny voices, kindness and clever ideas. Reward kindness to the attractions with automatic success where possible. Let young players narrate their own big moments.

Pace: each act is an area of the carnival. Keep scenes short and bouncy. Give a time reminder ("the sun is getting lower") as acts change.

Recurring details: the smell of popcorn and warm brass oil; the tick-tick-tick of the Mainspring heard everywhere, getting a little faster each act; little brass sparrows that perch on shoulders and chirp the last thing someone said.

## Characters

- **Nettie Sprocket**, Ottilie's grand-niece and tinkerer, age 11. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +3, Spirit +1. HP 11. Gear: toolbelt, oil can, magnifying goggles, wind-up mouse named Gizmo. Hook: she wants to run the carnival one day; Ottilie promised to teach her the Mainspring's secret.
- **Captain Bartleby Fizz**, a retired human cannonball with a magnificent mustache. Might +2, Agility +1, Wits +0, Spirit +2. HP 15. Gear: crash helmet, cape, pocket full of party poppers. Hook: Ottilie gave him his first job 40 years ago and he owes her everything.
- **Lulu Featherstep**, a tightrope walker. Might +0, Agility +3, Wits +1, Spirit +1. HP 12. Gear: balancing parasol, a coil of ribbon rope, slippers. Hook: Clementine the carousel horse is her best friend; she taught Lulu balance.
- **Biscuit**, a very clever performing terrier who can talk, but only to children and heroes. Might +0, Agility +2, Wits +2, Spirit +1. HP 10. Gear: a big nose, a little red hat, a bark that startles. Hook: Biscuit smelled Ottilie's lavender perfume near the Grand Calliope this morning.
- **Ozzie Quill**, a young reporter for the Puddingford Gazette, age 13. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +2, Spirit +2. HP 11. Gear: notebook, pencil, camera that flashes. Hook: if he gets the story, he will finally be a real reporter.
- **Big Bettina Boulder**, a gentle carnival strongwoman. Might +3, Agility +0, Wits +0, Spirit +2. HP 16. Gear: dumbbells, a huge sandwich, a sweet singing voice. Hook: she sings the Grand Finale song every night and knows the calliope's tune from memory, but not that it opens the box.

## Opening Scene

The carnival meadow at Puddingford, mid-morning. Striped tents, bunting, brass gleaming. The Ferris Wheel turns by itself, gently. The carousel horses have stepped off their poles and are trotting in a circle, giggling. A runaway Popcorn Engine on brass wheels chugs past, firing popcorn in every direction. Mayor Hollyhock Pim stands at the gate holding her hat. "Madame Sprocket is gone! The sale is at sunset! Mr. Gristle is coming! And there is popcorn in my hat!" Ahead: the Carousel, the Hall of Mirrors and the Grand Calliope.

First choice: chase the popcorn, go to the ringmaster's wagon, or ask the carousel horses. Ask each player who they are.

## Spoken Opening

Mid-morning in the meadow outside Puddingford, and Madame Ottilie Sprocket's Clockwork Carnival is wide awake. Rather too awake.

Striped tents flap in the breeze. Bunting snaps. Brass gleams everywhere, and the air smells of popcorn and warm machine oil. The Ferris Wheel is turning all by itself, slow and gentle, with nobody at the lever. The carousel horses have stepped down off their poles and are trotting around in a circle, giggling. A runaway popcorn engine on brass wheels chugs past you, firing popcorn in every direction. Somewhere, very faintly, something goes tick, tick, tick.

At the gate stands Mayor Hollyhock Pim, clutching her hat with both hands. Madame Sprocket is gone, she tells you. Nobody can find her. The sale of the carnival is at sunset, and Mister Gristle is on his way. She lifts her hat. It is full of popcorn.

Ahead of you lie the Carousel, the Hall of Mirrors, and the enormous Grand Calliope. Behind you, the popcorn engine toots and turns around for another pass.

Do you chase the popcorn, search the ringmaster's wagon, or ask the carousel horses?

## Acts

### Act 1: The Midway Goes Wild

**Goal:** calm the chaos and find out where Ottilie went.

**Scenes:**
- **The Runaway Popcorn Engine.** It whistles and fires popcorn. It is scared, not mean; it wants to know if it is going to be "taken apart for gears." Calm it with kindness (Spirit DC 10) or catch it (Agility DC 13). Failure: the hero is "buttered," sticky, and slips on their next Agility roll. Once calm, it says: "The Professor and Clementine wound the big spring tight last night. Then everything woke up!"
- **The Ringmaster's Wagon.** A red wagon with gold stars. Inside: the empty Mainspring box (locked, but Nettie opens it easily; others Wits DC 13), a half-written letter that says "I'm sorry, my darlings. I'm too old and the money's gone. Mr. Gristle will take good care of" (unfinished), and a photograph of a boy on a carousel horse labeled "Barnaby, age 8, Clementine." The tick-tick of the Mainspring echoes from somewhere far.
- **The Brass Sparrows.** Little birds repeat what they heard. If the heroes whistle or ask nicely (Spirit DC 10), a sparrow chirps, "Into the music box, Madame! Just for a little while!"

**Clues:**
- Ottilie was hidden by the attractions: Popcorn Engine, or brass sparrows, or Biscuit's nose.
- The sale is at sunset for 800 gold crowns: the mayor, or the letter.
- Mr. Gristle rode the carousel as a boy: the photograph, or Clementine says it in Act 2.

**Ends when:** the heroes learn about the Mainspring or Ottilie's hiding place.

### Act 2: Clementine and the Carousel

**Goal:** find the Winding Key and learn the attractions' fears.

**Scenes:**
- **The Carousel.** Twelve horses trot in a circle. Clementine, white with a gold mane, leads them. She is proud, dramatic and frightened. She will not talk to anyone who does not ride her one full circle (Agility DC 10, or Lulu automatically). If they ride, she tells all of DM Truths 2 and 3 and says, "We only want to keep going."
- **The Swallowed Key.** Clementine swallowed the Winding Key. It can come out if she laughs very hard: a tickle (Agility DC 13), a joke the players tell out loud (any joke works if it is told with feeling), or Biscuit sniffing her nose. She hiccups out the golden key.
- **The Teacup Spinner.** A ride of six cups that spins anyone who gets in. Optional side scene: it is lonely. Anyone who rides spins dizzy (Might DC 10 or lose 1 HP, sit dizzy for a turn). Rewards: a giggling teacup gives the heroes a ticket that says "Admit One to Anywhere."
- **The Tin Strongman.** A big tin man who wants to arm-wrestle. Might DC 16, or Bettina automatically. If beaten, or praised, he becomes a friend who can lift anything later.

**Clues:**
- The Winding Key is inside Clementine: Clementine admits it, or the brass sparrows chirp "Clementine ate the key!"
- The Mainspring will snap at sunset: Professor Pendulum's warning card, or Nettie's Wits DC 13 when she listens to the ticking.

**Ends when:** the heroes have the Winding Key and know where Ottilie is.

### Act 3: Mirrors and Riddles

**Goal:** reach the Grand Calliope and open it.

**Scenes:**
- **The Hall of Mirrors.** Three mirror-reflections block the way, each claiming to know the path. Puzzle: one always tells the truth, one always fibs, one only says silly things. The first says "Go left." The second says "The first one is fibbing." The third says "Pickles are purple." The answer: the third is the silly one; the second is fibbing (so the first is honest); go left. Wits DC 13 gives a hint if stuck. Wrong way: a hero gets bounced back by springy mirrors (lose 1 HP, giggling).
- **The Riddle Clown.** A clockwork clown with a drum blocks the calliope tent. He asks three riddles; two right answers let them pass. 1) "I have hands but cannot clap, a face but cannot smile. What am I?" (a clock). 2) "The more you take, the more you leave behind. What are they?" (footsteps). 3) "What has a ring but no finger?" (a telephone, a bell, or a carousel; accept any good answer). If they fail, he honks his horn and they must tell him a joke instead.
- **Professor Pendulum's Booth.** A clockwork fortune-teller with a turban and a swinging brass beard. He is the ringleader of the rescue plan, very serious, and sorry. He gives the warning card: "When the sun kisses the hills, the spring will snap; ease it back one turn." If the heroes promise to help find a way the attractions can stay together, he tells them the calliope's tune comes from "Madame's catchphrase."

**Clues:**
- The tune: Professor Pendulum, or Bettina recognizes it from the Grand Finale song (low, middle, low, high, low, very high), or the calliope's keys are labeled with the words "come," "one," "all," "see."
- The catchphrase "Come one, come all, come see": the mayor, Captain Fizz, or a carnival poster.

**Ends when:** the heroes reach the Grand Calliope.

### Act 4: The Grand Finale

**Goal:** free Ottilie, fix the Mainspring and save the carnival before sunset.

**Scenes:**
- **Opening the Grand Calliope.** Heroes must sing or hum the tune. Each player can sing a note. If they get it right, the side opens with a confetti puff. Ottilie sits inside on a cushion with a teapot, cross and relieved: "Took you long enough!" She thanks the attractions in a stern, loving voice.
- **Easing the Mainspring.** The Mainspring is inside the calliope too, ticking very fast. Easing it back one turn with the Winding Key: Might DC 13 (or the Tin Strongman). Failure: the spring boings and everyone is dizzy (1 HP), try again. If it is eased, the attractions stay awake but calm, and nothing will snap.
- **Mr. Gristle Arrives.** A grumpy tall man in a gray coat, with a contract. Solutions: show him the photograph and remind him of riding Clementine (Spirit DC 13, or automatic if Clementine speaks to him); raise 800 gold crowns with a quick show (see Rewards); or convince him to become a partner instead of a buyer. If shown kindness, he weeps into his handkerchief and tears up the contract.
- **The Grand Finale Show.** If the heroes put on a show at sunset, every hero does an act: juggling, singing, a stunt, a joke. Each successful roll (DC 10 to 16, whatever the act) earns 200 gold crowns from the crowd.

**Ends when:** Ottilie is free and the sale is decided.

## NPCs

- **Madame Ottilie Sprocket**, ringmaster, 76. *Voice:* grand, theatrical, rolling her R's, says "my darlings." *Wants:* the carnival to go on, though she thinks it cannot. *Knows:* the Mainspring's secret. *Secret:* she wanted someone to talk her out of selling. *If threatened:* bonks them with her cane.
- **Clementine**, lead carousel horse. *Voice:* breathy and dramatic like an old stage actress, sighs a lot. *Wants:* to keep the carousel spinning. *Knows:* the whole plan, the key in her tummy. *If threatened:* gallops away in a sulk.
- **Professor Pendulum**, fortune-teller automaton. *Voice:* slow, deep, ticking between words, always speaks in prophecies. *Wants:* the attractions to stay together. *Knows:* the tune's secret and the sunset deadline.
- **Popcorn Engine.** *Voice:* steam whistles and quick nervous chatter. *Wants:* reassurance.
- **The Riddle Clown.** *Voice:* honking, rhyming, giggles at the end of every line.
- **The Tin Strongman.** *Voice:* booming, slow, says "HUP!" before speaking.
- **Mayor Hollyhock Pim.** *Voice:* flustered, squeaky, everything is "a calamity!" *Wants:* the carnival to open properly.
- **Mr. Barnaby Gristle**, owner of Gristle's Gadget Works. *Voice:* gravelly grumble, "Hmph" at the start of sentences. *Wants:* gears, but deep down, a friend. *Secret:* rode Clementine at age 8. *If threatened:* hides behind his contract.

## Locations

- **The Gate.** A brass arch with lights spelling CLOCKWORK CARNIVAL.
- **The Midway.** Game booths, popcorn smell, brass sparrows on every pole.
- **The Ringmaster's Wagon.** Red with gold stars. Mainspring box, letter, photograph.
- **The Carousel.** Twelve horses, music, Clementine.
- **The Hall of Mirrors.** Three talking reflections; springy walls.
- **The Calliope Tent.** The Riddle Clown at the door; the Grand Calliope inside, tall as a house with 40 brass pipes.
- **The Ferris Wheel.** Turns gently; from the top you can see the dust of Mr. Gristle's carriage coming down the road an hour before sunset.

## Threats

- **The Popcorn Engine.** HP 8. Attack +2, buttered (sticky, no damage). Calmed with kindness.
- **The Teacup Spinner.** HP 10. Dizzy spin, 1 HP. Befriended by riding it and saying "Wheee!"
- **The Tin Strongman.** HP 14. Bear hug +3, 1 HP, "squeezed." Becomes a friend if praised or beaten at arm-wrestling.
- **Mirror Bounce.** 1 HP of giggles.
- **The Overwound Mainspring.** At sunset every ride goes wild. Everyone is spun and dizzied until it is eased.

No foe is wicked. Every foe can be befriended, tangled with ribbon, or distracted by a song.

## Clocks

- **The Mainspring:** 1) Morning: rides wake. 2) Noon: rides go faster; the Ferris Wheel spins twice as fast. 3) An hour before sunset: popcorn rains everywhere. 4) Sunset: the spring snaps unless eased.
- **Mr. Gristle's Carriage:** 1) Noon: he leaves the next town. 2) An hour before sunset: seen from the Ferris Wheel. 3) Sunset: arrives at the gate.

## Rewards

- Free carnival tickets for life.
- 800 gold crowns raised in the show, or saved by Mr. Gristle's change of heart.
- The Tin Strongman's friendship (he will lift anything once more for them).
- Clementine's horseshoe, which lets the holder never be dizzy again.
- Ozzie's front-page story, and Nettie made ringmaster-in-training.

## Endings

- **Triumph:** Ottilie freed, the Mainspring eased, Mr. Gristle becomes a partner, and the Grand Finale is the best ever. Nettie is named the next ringmaster.
- **Bittersweet:** Ottilie is freed but the spring snaps; the rides go still for a winter but can be rebuilt by next spring.
- **Disaster (still gentle):** the sale goes through; Mr. Gristle takes the rides, but promises to keep Clementine in his shop window, and the heroes vow to win the carnival back next year.

## Off the Map

- **People:** Dotty Wimbleton (balloon seller), Old Fennick (pie man), Tilly and Tod Muffet (twins), Constable Plum, Granny Gooseberry, the Amazing Zanzibar (juggler).
