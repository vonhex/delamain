# The Frostfall Pass

## Pitch

The town of Highmarrow lies on the far side of the Greyspine Mountains, and a winter fever is burning through it. The only cure is feverleaf, and the last caravan of the year carries six crates of it. You have been hired to get that caravan over the Frostfall Pass in five days, before the first great storm of winter seals the pass until spring. The snow has already started.

## DM Truths

1. The caravan belongs to Oswin Harrowgate, a merchant of the town of Thornwick. It carries six crates of feverleaf for Highmarrow, where the fever has already killed 30 people; without the feverleaf, roughly 200 more will die by spring. It also carries wool, salt and Oswin's savings.
2. The rival merchant house of Varrow wants the caravan to fail. If Highmarrow gets no medicine, the town will sell its silver mine to House Varrow next spring for a pittance. House Varrow's agent, Mistress Cordelia Varrow, paid the caravan's cook to sabotage the journey.
3. The traitor is Hobb Tennant, the cheerful cook. His family in Thornwick owes House Varrow 300 gold; Cordelia promised to cancel the debt and threatened to have his mother evicted if he refused. He is not a killer and feels sick about it. His sabotage, in order: on Day 1 he slits two food sacks so grain leaks out on the trail; on Day 2 he waters the lamp oil; on Day 3 he hangs rags soaked in deer blood from the last wagon to draw the wolves; on the night of Day 4 he plans to cut the harness of the medicine wagon (at the Saddle instead, if the caravan took the tunnel).
4. Hobb carries a letter from Cordelia in his boot, signed with a V in green wax: "Hold them past the fifth day. Your mother's house is safe as long as the medicine never arrives."
5. Something is hunting the caravan: a pack of seven rimewolves, white wolves of the high snows with frost on their fur, led by a huge grey-white she-wolf the mountain folk call Old Hoarfang. They follow because of the blood rags. Without the rags, they would lose interest by Day 4.
6. Higher up, in the Hag's Teeth caves, sleeps the Wintermaw, a frost troll ten feet tall with a beard of icicles. Loud noise in its caves wakes it. The shortcut tunnel through the Hag's Teeth saves a full day but passes its lair.
7. The storm is real. Maud Greyholt, the guide, reads the sky and knows it comes at dusk on Day 5. If the caravan is not past the Saddle (the summit) by then, the pass closes.
8. Two red herrings: Maud Greyholt is hiding that she is going snow-blind in her left eye; Lord Fennick Ashgrave, a passenger, is hiding that he deserted the King's army. Both act suspiciously, and both are innocent of sabotage.

## Table Notes

This is survival: cold, hunger, hard choices. Every day on the road gets one camp scene where the caravan talks around the fire; that is where the traitor can be found. Track food out loud ("you have four days of food left"); it makes the pressure real. Pace: roughly one act per day of travel, about 45 to 60 minutes each.

Content care: no one starves on screen. Mules and people can die in the cold or to wolves; describe it briefly and with dignity. Hobb is sympathetic; his discovery should feel like a tragedy, not a gotcha.

Recurring details: the creak of wagon wheels on packed snow; breath freezing on beards and scarves; the smell of pine smoke and Hobb's barley stew; the distant howl of the wolves every night, a little closer each time.

## Characters

- **Brannoc Steel**, a veteran caravan guard with a frostbitten ear. Might +3, Agility +1, Wits +0, Spirit +1. HP 16. Gear: greataxe (d10), shield, wolf-fur cloak. Hook: his brother lives in Highmarrow and has the fever.
- **Ysolde Fairwind**, a hunter and tracker from the mountain villages. Might +1, Agility +3, Wits +2, Spirit +0. HP 13. Gear: longbow (d8), 24 arrows, hunting knife (d6), snowshoes. Hook: rimewolves killed her father; she knows the name Old Hoarfang.
- **Brother Anselm**, a healer-monk of the High Shrine. Might +0, Agility +0, Wits +2, Spirit +3. HP 12. Gear: quarterstaff (d6), healing kit (5 uses, heal d6 each), prayer beads. Hook: he is going to Highmarrow to tend the sick, and the feverleaf will be his to brew.
- **Kestrel Dunmore**, a sharp young clerk sent by Highmarrow's council. Might +0, Agility +2, Wits +3, Spirit +1. HP 11. Gear: crossbow (d8), ledger of the caravan's goods, spyglass. Hook: Highmarrow sent her to make sure the medicine arrives; she suspects House Varrow wants it to fail.
- **Torvald Quarry**, a former miner, huge and quiet. Might +3, Agility +0, Wits +1, Spirit +1. HP 16. Gear: mining pick (d8), 50 feet of rope, two iron spikes, a lantern. Hook: he worked in the Hag's Teeth mines twenty years ago and saw something huge in the deep caves.

## Opening Scene

Dawn in the gate-yard of Thornwick, the last town before the mountains. Six wagons, twelve mules, frost on every rope. Oswin Harrowgate, plump and anxious, counts crates aloud. Hobb the cook passes out hot barley cakes with a grin. Maud Greyholt, the guide, squints at the grey sky and says: "Five days to Highmarrow. The big storm comes at dusk on the fifth. Not a minute later." The caravan has fourteen people and food for six days at full rations. Oswin asks the heroes to check the wagons before they leave. First choice: inspect the wagons, talk to the caravan folk, or head straight out.

## Spoken Opening

Dawn in the gate-yard of Thornwick, the last town before the mountains. Frost glitters on every rope and buckle. Six wagons stand in a line, and twelve mules stamp and blow clouds of steam into the cold.

Oswin Harrowgate, plump and anxious, walks the line counting crates aloud. Six of those crates hold feverleaf, the only cure for the fever burning through Highmarrow, on the far side of the Greyspine Mountains. Hobb, the cook, goes down the line with a basket and a grin, pressing hot barley cakes into every hand.

Maud Greyholt, the guide, stands apart with her eyes on the sky. It is low and grey. Five days to Highmarrow, she tells you. The great storm comes at dusk on the fifth day. Not a minute later.

Fourteen people. Food for six days, at full rations. Above the town, the Frostfall Pass climbs white into the clouds, and the first flakes of snow are already drifting down.

Oswin wrings his gloves and asks if you would check the wagons before you set out. Do you inspect the wagons, talk with the caravan folk, or head straight for the pass?

## Acts

### Act 1: The Lower Road (Days 1 and 2)

**Goal:** Get the caravan moving, learn who is who, and notice that something is wrong.

**Scenes:**
- **The caravan folk.** Fourteen people: the heroes (if fewer than five, add guards named Sigrun, Dace and Orrin to make up the number); Oswin; Hobb the cook; Maud the guide; Lord Fennick Ashgrave and his manservant Pell Rook; the drivers Big Annick, Hal Copper and Rosamund Teague; and a quiet girl of ten, Wren Harrowgate, Oswin's daughter. Each NPC has one thing to say around the fire (see NPCs).
- **Day 1, the Cairn Steps.** A steep zig-zag road past old stone cairns. A wheel slips on ice (Might DC 13 to hold the wagon; failure costs one crate of wool and an hour). At camp, the heroes find a trail of grain behind the food wagon. Two food sacks are slit. Food drops from 6 days to 5. Ysolde or Wits DC 13: the cuts are clean, from a sharp knife, not a rat.
- **Day 2, the Frozen Mere.** A frozen lake the road skirts. Crossing the ice saves two hours (Wits DC 13 to find a safe route; failure, a mule breaks through and drowns unless saved with Might DC 13). At dusk the first howl. Ysolde identifies rimewolves: seven of them.
- **Day 2 camp.** Lamps sputter and die: the oil has been watered. Torvald or Wits DC 10 tastes water in it. Only campfires now. Hobb's stew is excellent, as always.

**Clues:** Someone is sabotaging the caravan (the slit sacks; or the watered oil). Hobb had access to both (he packs the food and fills the lamps, Kestrel's ledger shows his signature for both; or Rosamund mentions she saw him at the oil barrel after dark).

**Ends when:** Day 2 camp ends with wolves howling close.

### Act 2: The Hollow Shrine (Day 3)

**Goal:** Survive the wolves and decide which route to take.

**Scenes:**
- **The Widow's Ledge, morning.** A narrow road cut into a cliff, wide enough for one wagon. Each wagon must be led (Agility or Might DC 10 per wagon; on a failure it slides and must be hauled back with Might DC 13, or lost with its cargo). If the medicine wagon is lost, the feverleaf must be carried by hand: each crate weighs as much as a man.
- **The wolf attack.** In the afternoon, Old Hoarfang and six rimewolves come down out of the pines and go straight for the last wagon. Ysolde or Wits DC 13 notices: they are sniffing the back of the last wagon. Under the tailboard hang three rags soaked in deer blood.
- **The Hollow Shrine.** An abandoned monks' hospice in a hollow under the Saddle: thick stone walls, a big hearth, a shrine to the Winter Saint with a frozen candle. The caravan can rest here safely; the wolves will not cross the threshold. Brother Anselm knows the place. On the shrine wall is carved a prayer: "Fire for the lost, bread for the hungry, and the truth before the snow."
- **The choice of road.** Maud explains: the high road over the Saddle takes two more days (Days 4 and 5), cutting it close to the storm. The Hag's Teeth tunnel is an old mining road through the mountain; it saves a day but Torvald knows something lives in the deep caves.

**Clues:** The blood rags were deliberate (the rags; or Ysolde finds deer tracks back near Day 2 camp, where someone killed a deer at night). Hobb is the traitor (blood on Hobb's sleeve, Wits DC 13; or Wren Harrowgate saw "Hobb went for a walk with his knife" last night; or Hobb's boot letter if searched, Agility DC 13). Red herrings: Maud stumbles and misjudges distances (she is going blind in one eye); Lord Fennick refuses to say where he served (he deserted).

**Ends when:** The caravan leaves the Hollow Shrine by one road or the other.

### Act 3: The High Road or the Hag's Teeth (Day 4)

**Goal:** Get through the hardest day.

**Scenes:**
- (High road) **The Bone Stair.** Switchbacks carved into a cliff, with old bones of mules from past caravans. Wind blasts (Might DC 13 each wagon or lose an hour). By dusk, food drops by one day as the cold doubles everyone's hunger.
- (High road) **Hobb's last sabotage.** At the night camp, Hobb sneaks out to cut the medicine wagon's harness. Anyone keeping watch (Wits DC 13) catches him in the act.
- (Hag's Teeth) **The tunnel mouth.** A carved archway between two jagged peaks. Old mining tracks, rusted carts, the smell of cold stone.
- (Hag's Teeth) **The Echo Gallery.** A cave where every sound echoes three times. Talking too loud or a dropped object wakes the Wintermaw (Agility DC 13 to move the wagons quietly; muffling the wheels with cloth gives +2).
- (Hag's Teeth) **The Wintermaw's lair.** A vast ice cavern, blue light through frozen walls. The Wintermaw sleeps curled around a pile of frozen mule bones. If woken, it roars and attacks. It can be distracted by food (a whole day of rations thrown down) or fire (it fears it and backs away from torches).
- (Hag's Teeth) **The far portal.** The tunnel ends at the Saddle, which the caravan reaches by noon of Day 4, a full day ahead.

**Clues:** Hobb's motive (his letter; or he confesses, weeping, when caught). The Wintermaw fears fire (Torvald remembers the miners kept torches lit; or Brother Anselm knows old stories of the Winter Saint driving trolls off with a candle).

**Ends when:** The caravan reaches the Saddle.

### Act 4: The Saddle and the Storm (Day 5)

**Goal:** Get over the summit and down to Highmarrow before the storm.

**Scenes:**
- **The Saddle.** The summit, a flat white field under a black sky. (High road: they arrive Day 5 morning. Tunnel: Day 4 noon, with a full day to spare, but Hobb's harness sabotage happens here that night.) Old Hoarfang's pack waits here if the blood rags were never removed, or if Ysolde's hunt brings her. Wind. Snow beginning.
- **Confronting Hobb.** If Hobb is still unexposed, he tries one last thing: running back down to signal Cordelia's men waiting at the Saddle with a horn. Two Varrow mercenaries, Gerrit and Ulla, have been shadowing the caravan; they will try to steal the medicine wagon.
- **The descent.** The road down to Highmarrow, a long slope. The storm hits at dusk. If the caravan is still on the Saddle, it is buried. If it is on the slope, a dash through the snow (Might DC 13 each hero, failure costs d6 HP to cold).
- **Highmarrow.** A walled town with a silver mine and tired guards. The sick are in the temple. Delivering the feverleaf saves the town.

**Ends when:** The caravan reaches Highmarrow, or the storm closes the pass.

## NPCs

- **Oswin Harrowgate**, merchant. *Voice:* fussy and anxious, counts things aloud in the middle of sentences. *Wants:* his goods delivered and his daughter safe. *Knows:* the cargo and the payment, 200 gold to the heroes on arrival. *Secret:* he is nearly broke; this trip is his last chance.
- **Hobb Tennant**, cook and traitor. *Voice:* jolly, hums while he works, calls everyone "my friend"; goes quiet and shaky when lying. *Wants:* his mother's house safe. *Knows:* the whole plot. *Secret:* the letter in his boot. *If caught:* breaks down at once; he will help fight the mercenaries if forgiven and promised help for his mother.
- **Maud Greyholt**, guide. *Voice:* terse, mountain-flat, spits before answering. *Wants:* one last crossing before she retires. *Secret:* going blind in her left eye. *If accused:* storms off, then admits the eye.
- **Lord Fennick Ashgrave**, passenger. *Voice:* aristocratic drawl, quotes poetry. *Wants:* to vanish in Highmarrow. *Secret:* he deserted the King's army after his company was ordered to burn a village. *If accused:* draws his sword, then lowers it and confesses.
- **Pell Rook**, Fennick's manservant. *Voice:* nervous whisper. *Knows:* Fennick's secret, and keeps it.
- **Wren Harrowgate**, Oswin's daughter, ten. *Voice:* bright and very literal. *Knows:* she saw Hobb leave camp with a knife on the night of Day 2.
- **Big Annick, Hal Copper, Rosamund Teague**, drivers. *Voices:* Annick booms; Hal stutters; Rosamund gossips. Rosamund saw Hobb at the oil barrel.
- **Cordelia Varrow**, agent of House Varrow (offstage until Highmarrow). *Voice:* silken and cold. *Wants:* the mine.
- **Gerrit and Ulla**, Varrow mercenaries. *Voices:* Gerrit grunts; Ulla laughs at everything.
- **Old Hoarfang**, she-wolf. No voice; a long low growl.

## Locations

- **Thornwick.** The gate-yard, the Last Lantern inn, the House Varrow counting-house.
- **The Cairn Steps.** Icy zig-zag road between stone cairns.
- **The Frozen Mere.** A lake of black ice, a shortcut across.
- **The Widow's Ledge.** One wagon wide, a sheer drop.
- **The Hollow Shrine.** Stone hospice, hearth, the Winter Saint's shrine with its prayer, a safe night.
- **The Bone Stair.** Wind-blasted switchbacks with old mule bones.
- **The Hag's Teeth.** Tunnel mouth, Echo Gallery, Wintermaw's ice lair, far portal.
- **The Saddle.** The summit field, black sky, the place the storm will bury.
- **Highmarrow.** Walled mining town, the fevered temple, the council hall.

## Threats

- **Rimewolves** (6). HP 8 each. Attack +3, d6 bite. Fear fire. Flee when: Old Hoarfang flees or three are down.
- **Old Hoarfang.** HP 18. Attack +5, d8 bite. Special: once per fight, knocks a hero prone. Flees at 6 HP. Can be turned aside without a fight by burning the blood rags and laying a deer carcass on the trail.
- **The Wintermaw.** HP 30. Attack +6, d10 fists. Special: its breath freezes (Agility DC 13 or d8 cold). Backs off from fire; takes double damage from torches. Distracted for an hour by a day's worth of food.
- **Gerrit and Ulla**, Varrow mercenaries. HP 12 each. Attack +4, d8 swords. Surrender when: one is down or Hobb turns on them.
- **The Cold.** Any night without fire costs every hero d6 HP.

## Clocks

- **The Storm:** 1) Day 3: the sky goes grey. 2) Day 4 morning: snow starts. 3) Day 5 noon: wind rises, visibility drops to ten feet. 4) Day 5 dusk: the storm hits and the pass closes.
- **Food (start: 6 days for 14 people):** 1) Day 1 slit sacks, -1 day. 2) Day 4 high road, -1 day. 3) Every 2 extra mouths (rescued travelers) eats a half day. 4) At zero, everyone takes d6 HP each day and rolls Spirit DC 13 or loses heart.
- **Hobb's Sabotage:** 1) Slit sacks. 2) Watered oil. 3) Blood rags. 4) Cut harness. Each step only happens if he is not caught first.

## Rewards

- Oswin's payment: 200 gold total on safe arrival, plus 50 if all six wagons survive.
- Highmarrow's gratitude: 300 gold from the council, and each hero is made a Freeman of Highmarrow (free lodging and trade forever).
- House Varrow's papers in Cordelia's satchel: proof of her plot, enough to have her arrested.
- Old Hoarfang's pelt, if she falls: worth 80 gold, and gives +1 on rolls against the cold.

## Endings

- **Triumph:** The caravan crosses with the feverleaf, Hobb is found out and forgiven, Cordelia is arrested, and the fever breaks. Highmarrow keeps its mine.
- **Bittersweet:** The medicine arrives but late or partly lost, or someone dies on the way. Half the sick are saved. Hobb is hanged or flees, and his mother is evicted anyway.
- **Disaster:** The storm closes the pass with the caravan on the Saddle. They survive in the Hollow Shrine through winter, and Highmarrow sells the mine to House Varrow in spring.
