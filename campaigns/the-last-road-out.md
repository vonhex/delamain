# The Last Road Out

## Pitch

Twenty-six years after the Burning, the town of Kettle Flats has run out of water. Thirty-one people, a school bus, an armored pickup, a fuel tanker and one motorcycle are leaving for Meridian, a walled city on a lake 840 miles to the west, which nobody in the convoy has ever seen. You are the ones they are counting on to get them there.

## DM Truths

1. Meridian is real. It is a walled city of 4,000 people on the shore of Coldwater Lake, run by a council and guarded by Warden Abel Strand. It is nearly full and its water purifiers are failing. Newcomers are only admitted if they bring a "gift" the city needs.
2. The convoy carries the Still, a solar water purifier the size of a refrigerator, built by engineer Dr. Ines Calloway. It turns 400 liters of foul water clean a day. It rides on the roof rack of the bus, Big Mother. It is the only working one anyone knows of outside Meridian.
3. Fuel is counted in drums. The convoy starts with 12 drums in the tanker, Dolly. The trip needs 13: Kettle Flats to the Saltglass Pan, 3 drums; the Pan to Carver's Gorge, 3 drums; the Gorge across the Glass Mile, 4 drums; the last stretch to Meridian, 3 drums. They are one drum short and must find more.
4. The bridge over Carver's Gorge collapsed last spring. The only crossing is the road on top of Hollis Dam, held by 60 people under Marta Hollis. Their purifier broke in summer; four of their children are sick from bad water. Marta will ask for the Still as the price of crossing.
5. The Red Wheel is a raider gang of about 40 who control the Glass Mile. Their leader is Kade Roan, the older brother of the hero Silas Roan; Kade left Kettle Flats nine years ago. The Red Wheel are mostly people Meridian turned away at the gate. They want fuel. Kade's real wish is to get his people somewhere safe too.
6. Meridian's council will grant entry to all 31 if they bring the Still. Without it, Warden Strand will admit only 20, chosen by the council for useful skills.
7. The hidden way through: Ines Calloway herself is the real gift. She can build new Stills in Meridian's workshop, one every six weeks. If she offers to stay and work, and the heroes argue well, Meridian takes everyone and can send a Still back to Hollis Dam and even make room for some Red Wheel families. Ines will only realize this if someone asks her what she could build with a real workshop.
8. Ines's daughter Ada, age 9, has a cough that is getting worse; she needs the antibiotic tablets in the old clinic at Last Chance, or Hana's medical kit and rest.

## Table Notes

The feel is a road movie: miles, engine noise, campfires, hard bargains. Track fuel aloud ("Dolly's down to seven drums"). Make the convoy feel like people, not cargo: give names, show children in the bus windows. Violence happens, but keep it quick and not lingering; deaths of named NPCs only when choices earn them. The central choice is moral: who gets clean water. Do not tell players the "right" answer; let every option cost something.

Pace: one act per leg of the trip. Between acts, a short campfire scene lets the heroes talk and plan.

Recurring details: red dust that gets into everything; the radio that crackles between stations, sometimes catching a far-off woman's voice reading Meridian's admission rules; Big Mother's horn, a two-tone honk that kids love.

Vehicle rules: Big Mother (bus) HP 30, Rattler (armored pickup) HP 25, Dolly (tanker) HP 20 (every 5 damage spills 1 drum of fuel), Juniper (motorcycle) HP 10. Driving rolls use Agility; repairs use Wits. A vehicle at 0 HP stops until repaired (Wits DC 13, one hour).

## Characters

- **Rosa Vance**, driver of Big Mother and a veteran of a hundred dust storms. Might +1, Agility +3, Wits +1, Spirit +0. HP 13. Gear: tire iron (d6), toolkit, the bus's spare key on a chain. Hook: the 22 people on her bus are her responsibility, including her old father Hector.
- **Silas Roan**, scout on the motorcycle Juniper. Might +0, Agility +2, Wits +2, Spirit +1. HP 12. Gear: pistol (d6, 12 shots), binoculars, road atlas. Hook: his older brother Kade left nine years ago; Silas has heard Kade leads the Red Wheel.
- **Hana Mori**, the convoy's medic. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +2, Spirit +2. HP 11. Gear: medical kit (heals d6 HP, 5 uses), scalpel (d6), a lucky photograph of the ocean. Hook: she is treating Ada's cough and fears it is pneumonia.
- **Tobias "Tank" Okoro**, gunner on the Rattler. Might +3, Agility +0, Wits +0, Spirit +1. HP 16. Gear: shotgun (d8), crowbar (d6), welding torch. Hook: he swore to Ines Calloway that he would keep the Still safe at any cost.
- **Pilar Esk**, a 16-year-old salvager with quick fingers. Might +0, Agility +3, Wits +1, Spirit +1. HP 10. Gear: slingshot (d6), crowbar, sack, a hand-drawn list of places worth looting. Hook: Last Chance truck stop is on her list, marked "fuel underground."

## Opening Scene

Dawn at Kettle Flats. The town well is dry and the last cottonwood is dead. Four vehicles are lined up on the main road: Big Mother, painted yellow under the rust, the Still strapped to her roof under a tarp; the Rattler, welded with steel plates; Dolly, the tanker, ticking as she warms; and Juniper, the motorcycle. Convoy leader June Harker, gray braid, rifle on her shoulder, climbs the bus steps and says, "Eight hundred forty miles. Twelve drums. Thirty-one of us. Nobody gets left." Children wave from the bus windows. A dust devil spins on the horizon.

First choice: which vehicle each hero rides, and who scouts ahead. Ask each player who they are.

## Spoken Opening

Dawn at Kettle Flats, twenty-six years after the Burning. The town well is dry. The last cottonwood tree stands dead and white beside the road. Red dust lies over everything.

Four vehicles wait in a line on the main street. Big Mother, the school bus, painted yellow beneath the rust, with the Still, the convoy's water purifier, strapped to her roof under a tarp. The Rattler, an armored pickup welded with steel plates. Dolly, the fuel tanker, ticking softly as her engine warms. And Juniper, a lean motorcycle, nose pointed west.

June Harker, the convoy leader, gray braid down her back and a rifle on her shoulder, climbs the steps of the bus and turns to face you all. Eight hundred and forty miles, she says. Twelve drums of fuel. Thirty-one people. Nobody gets left.

Children press their faces to the bus windows and wave. Big Mother sounds her two-tone horn, and they cheer. Far off on the horizon, a dust devil spins and wanders.

Somewhere out there, beyond the salt and the glass, is Meridian. Nobody here has ever seen it.

Which vehicle do you ride, and who scouts ahead?

## Acts

### Act 1: The Saltglass Pan

**Goal:** cross 200 miles of salt flats and find extra fuel.

**Scenes:**
- **Leaving Town.** Old Hector Vance refuses to leave his house; Spirit DC 13 or Rosa automatically talks him onto the bus. If left, he follows on foot and is found an hour out.
- **The Dust Storm.** A wall of red dust hits at mile 120. Every driver rolls Agility DC 13. Failure: the vehicle takes d8 damage hitting a wreck. Scout on Juniper can guide them through with Wits DC 13, giving everyone +2.
- **Last Chance.** A dead truck stop at mile 180: a gas station, a diner, a small clinic. Pilar's list says fuel is underground. Wits DC 13 finds the old tank hatch; Might DC 13 to pry it; a hand pump yields 2 drums. The clinic has a jar of 20 antibiotic tablets (cures Ada) in a locked drawer (Agility DC 13 or crowbar).
- **The Dust Dogs.** While they pump, six feral dogs stalk out of the diner. They can be fed (any food), scared with fire or noise (Spirit DC 13), or fought.

**Clues:**
- There is extra fuel at Last Chance: Pilar's list, or Silas's binoculars spotting the old fuel sign.
- Meridian requires a gift: the radio broadcast (Wits DC 10 to tune it), or June Harker mentions rumors.

**Ends when:** the convoy camps on the far side of the Pan. Fuel: 12 minus 3, plus 2 if they pumped, so 11.

### Act 2: Hollis Dam

**Goal:** cross Carver's Gorge.

**Scenes:**
- **The Fallen Bridge.** Mile 380. The great bridge lies in the river 300 feet below. Silas scouting finds the only way across: Hollis Dam, 10 miles north.
- **The Gate on the Dam.** A barricade of cars, six guards with rifles. Marta Hollis, a tall woman in a welder's apron, meets them. She asks what they carry. She sees the Still under the tarp (or someone tells her) and says, "That's the price. Our kids are sick."
- **The Sick Children.** Four children in the dam's infirmary, fevered from bad water. Hana can treat them (Wits DC 13, uses 1 kit use each) and buy goodwill. Hana or Ines can confirm the water is the cause.
- **Bargaining.** Marta will accept: the Still; or 4 drums of fuel and a promise to send help from Meridian; or a day's repair work by Ines on their old purifier (it works for a month). Spirit DC 13 to talk her down to 2 drums. If the heroes treat the children, she accepts the promise alone.
- **Ines at the Campfire.** If asked what she could do with a proper workshop, Ines says, "I could build a Still every six weeks. Maybe faster with help." This plants DM Truth 7.

**Clues:**
- The Still is the gift Meridian wants: the radio broadcast repeats, "Water technology will be valued," or Marta says Meridian took her brother's purifier as his entry price years ago.
- Ines can build more: Ines at the campfire, or Tank remembers her saying it once.

**Ends when:** the convoy crosses the dam. Fuel: minus 3, plus or minus what was bargained.

### Act 3: The Glass Mile

**Goal:** get through Red Wheel territory.

**Scenes:**
- **The Glass Mile.** A stretch where a city melted in the Burning. The road is fused glass, sharp and slick. Each driver rolls Agility DC 13 or takes d6 tire damage.
- **The Red Wheel Ambush.** Twelve raiders on bikes and a war truck called the Ox block the road. Their leader, Kade Roan, red scarf, scarred face, demands 4 drums of fuel as toll.
- **Brothers.** If Silas speaks to Kade, Kade lowers his gun. He says the Red Wheel are folk Meridian turned away. If Silas or another hero promises to speak for them at Meridian, Kade lets them pass for 1 drum. Spirit DC 13.
- **The Chase.** If they refuse or fight: a running battle. Each round, drivers roll Agility DC 13 to keep ahead; raiders attack the Dolly first. Kade calls off the attack if the Ox is disabled or if Silas is hurt.
- **Night Camp.** Kade may visit the camp alone that night, if Silas talked to him. He tells Silas, "Bring us in too, little brother. Or don't come back this way."

**Clues:**
- The Red Wheel are exiles: Kade says it, or a raider captive confesses.
- Meridian's gate rules: Kade knows them exactly, "Twenty in without a gift, all in with one."

**Ends when:** they pass the Glass Mile. Fuel: minus 4, minus toll.

### Act 4: The Gate of Meridian

**Goal:** get the convoy into Meridian, and decide who gets clean water.

**Scenes:**
- **The Last Stretch.** 200 miles. If fuel runs out, vehicles must be abandoned; Big Mother can carry 30 people if crammed. On the last ridge, they see Coldwater Lake, blue and real, and white walls with green fields inside.
- **The Gate.** Warden Abel Strand and twenty guards. Strand is calm, tired, fair. "What is your gift?"
- **The Choice.** If they have the Still: everyone enters. If they gave it away: only 20 enter, the heroes must choose. If someone offers Ines as the gift (Ines will agree if asked; she asks that a Still be sent to Hollis Dam), roll Spirit DC 16 to persuade the council to take all 31; Ines's offer plus Spirit DC 13 for the Red Wheel families too.
- **Ada.** If Ada is still sick, Strand admits her immediately to the city's hospital no matter what else happens.

**Ends when:** the gate opens or closes.

## NPCs

- **June Harker**, convoy leader, 62. *Voice:* flat, dry, every sentence short, ends orders with "Understood?" *Wants:* nobody left behind. *Knows:* the route and the fuel math. *Secret:* she is going blind and cannot drive. *If threatened:* raises her rifle and stays calm.
- **Dr. Ines Calloway**, engineer, 41. *Voice:* fast, technical, distracted, interrupts herself with "wait, no, listen." *Wants:* Ada safe. *Knows:* how the Still works. *Secret:* she could build more. *If threatened:* stands in front of the Still.
- **Ada Calloway**, 9. *Voice:* small, coughing, asks "are we there yet?" *Wants:* to see the lake.
- **Hector Vance**, Rosa's father, 80. *Voice:* grumbling, old stories, "back before the Burning." *Knows:* old roads.
- **Marta Hollis**, leader of Hollis Dam. *Voice:* low, steady, welder's rasp, never smiles. *Wants:* clean water for her children. *Knows:* Meridian's rules. *Secret:* her brother is inside Meridian. *If threatened:* her guards open fire.
- **Kade Roan**, Red Wheel leader. *Voice:* slow and mocking, calls Silas "little brother." *Wants:* safety for his 40. *Knows:* Meridian's gate rules. *Secret:* he misses home. *If threatened:* fights hard, retreats if the Ox is lost.
- **Warden Abel Strand.** *Voice:* soft and measured, a teacher's patience, says "I hear you" before every refusal. *Wants:* Meridian to survive. *Knows:* the council's needs. *Secret:* he hates turning people away.

## Locations

- **Kettle Flats.** Dry well, dead cottonwood, empty houses.
- **The Saltglass Pan.** White flats, wrecks half-buried in salt, mirages.
- **Last Chance.** Truck stop, buried tank with 2 drums, clinic with 20 antibiotic tablets, dust dogs.
- **Carver's Gorge.** Collapsed bridge, 300-foot drop.
- **Hollis Dam.** Barricade, infirmary, 60 people, broken purifier.
- **The Glass Mile.** Melted city, glassy road, Red Wheel camp in a burned stadium.
- **Meridian.** White walls, lake, green fields, 4,000 people.

## Threats

- **Dust Dogs** (6). HP 5 each. Attack +2, d6. Flee if two fall or if fed.
- **Red Wheel Raiders** (12). HP 8 each. Attack +3, d6. Flee when Kade calls retreat.
- **The Ox** (war truck). HP 30. Ram +4, d10 to a vehicle.
- **Kade Roan.** HP 14. Attack +4, d8. Yields to Silas.
- **Hollis Guards** (6). HP 8. Attack +3, d6. Won't fight if children are helped.
- **Dust Storm, Glass Mile.** Agility DC 13 or vehicle damage d6 to d8.

## Clocks

- **Fuel:** 12 drums start. Count it each leg. With the Last Chance drums and no tolls: 11 after Act 1, 8 after Act 2, 4 after Act 3, 1 left at Meridian. Every drum paid in tolls or spilled from Dolly must be made up: abandoning the Rattler saves 1 drum per remaining leg, abandoning Juniper saves nothing. At 0, vehicles stop; the convoy walks, about 20 miles a day, and Ada's Cough clock advances one step per day.
- **Ada's Cough:** 1) Act 1 coughing. 2) Act 2 fever. 3) Act 3 struggling to breathe. 4) Act 4 critical. Antibiotics or Hana's care resets it.
- **Red Wheel Patience:** 1) They watch from ridges in Act 2. 2) They block the road. 3) They attack. 4) If the convoy goes back, they ambush at the dam.

## Rewards

- 2 drums at Last Chance; 20 antibiotic tablets.
- Marta's friendship (Hollis guards escort them to the Glass Mile).
- Kade's peace (Red Wheel let them pass).
- Inside Meridian: homes, clean water, Ines's workshop.

## Endings

- **Triumph:** all 31 enter Meridian; Ines builds Stills; one goes back to Hollis Dam; the Red Wheel families are admitted.
- **Bittersweet:** the convoy enters with the Still while Hollis Dam suffers; or 20 enter and 11 are turned away.
- **Disaster:** the fuel runs out or the Red Wheel takes Dolly, and the convoy is stranded on the Glass Mile.

## Off the Map

- **People:** Grady Puckett (trader), Sister Moon (wandering healer), the Tolliver twins (scavengers), Abe Lusk (hermit mechanic), Corazon Day (radio woman).
