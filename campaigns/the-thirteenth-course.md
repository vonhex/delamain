# The Thirteenth Course

## Pitch

Every midwinter, the Countess of Morrow Fell serves twelve courses, and sets a thirteenth plate under silver for the family's dead. Tonight the snow has buried the road. Between the seventh course and the eighth a guest is found dead in the library, and by midnight, when the thirteenth plate is uncovered, someone at this table must answer for it.

## DM Truths

1. Morrow Fell Hall is a cold stone manor on the Brannoch Moors. Tonight is the winter solstice. A blizzard closed the only road at 6:00 in the evening; nobody arrives or leaves until morning.
2. The victim is Lord Tobias Fennick, 58, a moneylender lord, poisoned with tincture of monkshood (aconite), a liniment for external use. It numbs the mouth within minutes and stops the heart within half an hour.
3. The murderer is Doctor Anselm Graye, the county physician. Two years ago, drunk, Graye gave the late Earl Varrick, the Countess's husband, a triple dose of laudanum that killed him. Fennick was in the house and saw, and has blackmailed Graye since. Last week he demanded 500 crowns by Candlemas, which Graye cannot pay.
4. At the venison pie (8:20) Fennick complained loudly of indigestion. At 9:03, during the juniper sorbet, Graye, at Fennick's left, offered "my digestive drops" and tipped a small brown bottle over his sorbet: the monkshood, not peppermint.
5. At 9:12 Fennick said to Graye, quietly but heard by Philippa Thorne at his right: "My lips are numb, Graye. What did you give me?" Graye answered: "Peppermint, Tobias. It numbs. Walk it off."
6. At 9:15 the Long Pause began (the Countess's 20-minute interval before the roast). Fennick, already sweating and unsteady, went to the library to meet the Countess, as arranged by note.
7. At 9:20 the Countess entered and begged him to extend the 12,000 crown mortgage on the Hall, due at New Year. He refused; they argued loudly. She left at 9:25. He was alive, gray, clutching his chest; she thought him drunk.
8. At 9:28 Graye entered to take the blackmail letter. Fennick, collapsed by the fire, died while Graye turned out his coat pockets. Graye missed the letter, hidden in the lid of Fennick's snuffbox, and left at 9:31.
9. At 9:35 the bell rang for the roast swan. Footman Ambrose Kettle was sent for the empty chair and found Fennick dead at 9:38.
10. Graye claims he smoked in the conservatory all through the Pause. It has been locked since Tuesday, when frost cracked the glass roof; the only key is on the housekeeper's ring, which never left her.
11. Graye believes Philippa heard him at 9:12. At 11:00 he sends her hot chocolate laced with the last of the tincture (see Clocks).
12. Housekeeper Hester Quill nursed the late Earl and has suspected Graye for two years. Under the silver dome of the thirteenth plate she has put a card: "For the Earl, from Anselm Graye, who owes him." At midnight the Countess lifts it, unless the heroes act first.

The exact timeline of the evening:
- 6:00 road closes in the snow. 7:00 guests seated, course 1, oysters. 7:20 course 2, chestnut soup. 7:40 course 3, trout in butter. 8:00 course 4, roast quail. 8:20 course 5, venison pie (Fennick complains of indigestion). 8:40 course 6, cheese and pears. 9:00 course 7, juniper sorbet. 9:03 Graye doses the sorbet. 9:12 "My lips are numb." 9:15 Long Pause begins, Fennick goes to the library. 9:20 to 9:25 the Countess in the library. 9:28 to 9:31 Graye in the library. 9:35 bell for course 8, roast swan. 9:38 body found. The Countess insists the feast goes on ("We do not stop the feast"): 10:00 course 9, salmon; 10:30 course 10, game broth; 11:00 course 11, plum pudding; 11:30 course 12, sugared violets and port. Midnight: the thirteenth course.

The suspects, with alibi, motive, lie and tell:
- **Doctor Anselm Graye (the murderer).** Motive: blackmail over the Earl's death. Alibi: smoking alone in the conservatory 9:15 to 9:35. Lie: it is locked, his silver cigar case still holds all six cigars, and he smells of no smoke. Tell: cleans his spectacles. Walks with a silver-tipped cane: tap, tap.
- **Countess Isolde Varrick, host.** Motive: Fennick holds the mortgage on Morrow Fell, 12,000 crowns due at New Year. Alibi: praying in the chapel. Lie: she was in the library 9:20 to 9:25. Tell: twists her wedding ring. She lies because she fears the rope.
- **Captain Rhys Valden, retired cavalry officer.** Motive: Fennick jilted his sister Clara ten years ago and ruined her name. Alibi: billiards with Corwin 9:15 to 9:35. Lies: he covers for Corwin, who left 9:22 to 9:32 (so Valden was alone then); and he says he "saw nothing at the table", though he saw Graye tip a brown bottle over the sorbet. He shields Graye, his old regimental surgeon, who saved half his leg. Tell: his wooden leg thumps as he shifts weight, and he shifts when lying.
- **Miss Philippa Thorne, Fennick's ward, 19.** Motive: she was to be engaged to Fennick at midnight, against her will. Alibi: writing in her room. Lie: she was in the stable passage with Corwin 9:22 to 9:32. Tell: quotes poetry when evasive. She heard the 9:12 words but did not understand them.
- **Corwin Varrick, the Countess's son, 24.** Motive: owes Fennick 900 crowns in gambling debts, and loves Philippa. Alibi: billiards the whole Pause. Lie: left 9:22 to 9:32 to meet Philippa. Tell: laughs too loudly.
- **Hester Quill, housekeeper.** Not truly a suspect. Holds every key. Knows Graye was drunk the night the Earl died.

## Table Notes

Candlelit, cold, polite. Every suspect hides something; only one hid a murder. Play every lie consistently. Never reveal the culprit; the players must accuse. Fade out the body's suffering: say "he lies still by the hearth, his face gray". Pace: Act 1 the feast and the death (40 min), Act 2 the investigation (75), Act 3 the second cup (60), Act 4 the thirteenth course (45).

Recurring details: wind moaning in the chimneys. The empty thirteenth chair and its silver dome. Juniper, in the sorbet and the boughs on the walls. The hall clock striking each quarter hour.

## Characters

- **Lady Merrin Ashcombe**, sharp-eyed widow and amateur sleuth. Might +0, Agility +1, Wits +3, Spirit +1. HP 11. Gear: lorgnette, notebook, hatpin (d6). Hook: Fennick took her townhouse over her late husband's debts, which makes her a suspect too.
- **Brother Calloway Dunne**, traveling friar and herbalist, sheltering from the snow. Might +1, Agility +0, Wits +2, Spirit +2. HP 13. Gear: herb satchel, rosary, walking staff (d6). Hook: the Countess's confessor; two years ago she told him she doubted her husband died naturally. Knows monkshood by smell (Wits DC 10).
- **Sergeant Odile Harrow**, off-duty county constable, guest of Captain Valden. Might +2, Agility +1, Wits +1, Spirit +1. HP 14. Gear: truncheon (d6), handcuffs, warrant card. Hook: the only law in the house; Valden is her old friend and she does not want it to be him.
- **Tobin Fairweather**, hired fiddler for the feast. Might +0, Agility +3, Wits +1, Spirit +1. HP 12. Gear: fiddle, bow, small knife (d6). Hook: eats with the servants and hears their gossip; Agnes the maid is his sweetheart.
- **Miss Junia Coldridge**, Fennick's niece and heir. Might +1, Agility +1, Wits +1, Spirit +2. HP 12. Gear: her uncle's pocket watch, a small pistol (d6, one shot, then a long reload). Hook: inherits everything, so she is the obvious suspect. Knows her uncle "kept his secrets in his snuffbox."

## Opening Scene

7:00 in the evening. Snow hisses at tall windows; two hundred candles light the great hall. Thirteen chairs at the long table: at the head, the Countess, in gray silk; at the foot, an empty chair with a silver-domed plate. She raises a glass: "Twelve courses for the living, the thirteenth for those we have lost. And tonight nobody leaves at all; the road is gone." Oysters arrive. Seating, going down the table from the Countess: Corwin, Philippa, Fennick, Graye, Valden, then the heroes; Junia sits directly across from her uncle. Philippa is at Fennick's right hand and Graye at his left. Ask each player who they are, then play the courses quickly: Fennick is loud and drunk, complains of indigestion at the venison, and at the sorbet a hero may notice (Wits DC 16) Graye lean toward his dish.

## Acts

### Act 1: The Feast and the Fall

**Goal:** enjoy the feast, notice things, react to the death.

**Scenes:**
- **Courses 1 to 7.** Small talk. Fennick promises "something delightful at midnight" and squeezes Philippa's hand; she goes white. He calls the Countess's roof "my roof, soon". He jokes to Graye: "Candlemas, Doctor, don't forget." Graye cleans his spectacles.
- **The Long Pause.** At 9:15 guests scatter. A hero following Fennick sees him enter the library, sweating. One following Graye sees him head for the conservatory corridor, then double back toward the library at 9:27 (Agility DC 13 to stay unseen).
- **The Body.** At 9:38 Ambrose Kettle screams. Fennick lies dead by the library hearth. The Countess lets the heroes investigate until midnight, but the feast continues.

**Clues:**
- Fennick was ill before the library: his sweating at the sorbet (any hero at table), or Agnes saw him stagger in at 9:15.
- Fennick had a hold over Graye ("Candlemas, Doctor"): heard at the table, or Junia remembers her uncle saying "the doctor pays on time".

**Ends when:** the body is found and someone starts asking questions.

### Act 2: The Library and the Lies

**Goal:** examine the body and room, and question everyone.

**Scenes:**
- **The Library.** Fennick by the fire. Coat pockets turned inside out. His silver snuffbox on the rug. A full, untouched glass of brandy (clean). The Countess's ivory fan under the armchair. Hester waxed the floor this afternoon: near the body are coin-sized round dents from a cane's silver tip, and none of the large flat dents a wooden leg makes.
- **The Snuffbox.** A hidden compartment in the lid (Wits DC 13, or automatic if Junia mentions his secrets) holds Fennick's copy of a letter: "G. Five hundred crowns by Candlemas, or the Countess learns how the Earl truly died. F."
- **The Body Itself.** Calloway or anyone with Wits DC 13 smells a sharp, bitter, mousy herb on his lips: monkshood. Calloway knows it kills within half an hour, so Fennick was dosed between 8:45 and 9:15, at the table.
- **Agnes Pike, the Maid.** Polished the corridor outside the library all Pause. Talks freely to Tobin; others need Spirit DC 13. She saw Fennick go in at 9:15. Heard a woman shouting 9:20 to 9:25: "You will have the house by spring and I will have nothing!" Saw a lady in gray silk leave at 9:25 (the Countess wears gray; Philippa wears white; Junia wears green). Then Agnes stepped into the linen closet; at 9:28 she heard tap, tap, a cane, go into the library, and at 9:31 tap, tap, go away. "Not the Captain. The Captain goes thump."
- **Interrogations.** Proof of a lie makes a suspect confess the truth: the Countess admits the argument when shown her fan or Agnes's account; Corwin and Philippa admit their meeting when either is caught out or Nate tells; Valden admits the brown bottle when shown the cane dents or told about Philippa's "lips are numb" (Spirit DC 13 otherwise, DC 10 if Sergeant Harrow asks).
- **Graye's Bag.** In his room, or brought to "help examine the body": a peppermint drops bottle with its wax seal unbroken, and a brown bottle labeled "Tinct. Aconiti. For external use only", nearly empty. Graye claims he used it on Valden's leg last week; Valden says Graye has not touched his leg since the war.

**Clues:**
- Monkshood was the poison: Calloway smells it on the body, or the near-empty bottle in Graye's bag.
- Dosed at the table during the sorbet: the timing from Calloway, or Valden's confession, or the Kitchen scene in Act 3.
- Graye's motive: the snuffbox letter, or the valet Jory Mabb ("His lordship wrote to the doctor about the Earl's last night").
- Graye was in the library at 9:28: Agnes's cane tap, or the cane dents in the waxed floor.
- Graye's alibi is false: the conservatory is locked (Hester, or trying the door: frozen and locked), or his cigar case holds all six cigars and he smells of no smoke.
- The Countess lied about the chapel: Agnes's gray silk and her fan, or the chapel's unlit candle and dusty kneeler.

**Ends when:** the heroes have spoken to at least four suspects and examined the library, or the 11:00 clock strikes.

### Act 3: The Second Cup

**Goal:** follow the poison back to the table, and stop Graye from killing again.

**Scenes:**
- **The Kitchen.** Cook Mrs. Ida Bramble is furious. The sorbet dishes went to the scullery at 9:15; the cat Pewter licked Fennick's ("the one with his crest on the spoon") and has staggered and drooled since 9:30; it will live, just. Calloway confirms monkshood.
- **The Stable Passage.** Cold, smelling of horses. Stable boy Nate Ruddock saw Corwin and Philippa here 9:22 to 9:32, kissing, "so they never killed nobody". Exposed, Philippa recounts the 9:12 words: "My lips are numb, Graye. What did you give me?"
- **The Locked Conservatory.** Hester unlocks it: frost inside the glass, a cracked roof pane, dead orchids, snow on the floor with no footprints. Nobody stood here tonight.
- **The Second Cup.** At 11:00 a maid carries a cup of hot chocolate to Philippa "from the doctor, for her nerves". Intercepted, it smells of monkshood (bitter, mousy). If she drinks it, she falls ill at 11:10; a hero saves her with Wits DC 13 (Calloway DC 10), or she dies at 11:40.
- **Graye Cornered.** Accused with three or more clues, Graye cleans his spectacles and confesses quietly: the Earl, the blackmail. With fewer, he denies smoothly and blames the Countess. Caught, he flees into the blizzard (Agility DC 13 to catch him) or grabs Philippa with a scalpel (d6) and demands a horse.

**Ends when:** the heroes accuse someone, or the clock strikes midnight.

### Act 4: The Thirteenth Course

**Goal:** name the murderer before the household, and live with what comes out.

**Scenes:**
- **Midnight at the Table.** The clock strikes twelve. The Countess lifts the dome over the thirteenth plate. On it, Hester's card: "For the Earl, from Anselm Graye, who owes him." The Countess turns to Hester; Hester tells everyone Graye was drunk the night the Earl died.
- **The Accusation.** Named with evidence, Graye confesses or flees. If they name someone else, Graye eagerly helps convict them; only Hester's card can save the innocent.
- **The Snow Chase.** Graye rides into the blizzard. Two successes at Might DC 13 through the drifts catch him; otherwise he is found frozen at dawn by the gatehouse.
- **The Morning.** Dawn; the road opens. Harrow takes Graye to the county town. Junia decides whether to keep the Countess's mortgage.

**Ends when:** the culprit is named and taken, escapes, or an innocent is accused.

## NPCs

Suspects' alibis, lies and tells are in DM Truths.

- **Doctor Anselm Graye**, county physician, 61. *Voice:* soft, slow, reassuring, calls everyone "my dear". *Wants:* the letter destroyed and Philippa silenced. *Secret:* the murder and the Earl. *If threatened:* calm denial; if cornered, flight or a hostage.
- **Countess Isolde Varrick**, host, 52. *Voice:* cool, formal, speaks of herself as "one". *Wants:* to keep the Hall. *If threatened:* icy silence, then tears.
- **Captain Rhys Valden**, 55. *Voice:* barking, short military sentences, calls everyone "sir" or "madam". *Wants:* to protect Graye. *If threatened:* stands to attention and says "Do your worst."
- **Miss Philippa Thorne**, 19. *Voice:* breathless, dramatic, quotes poems ("The frost performs its secret ministry"). *Wants:* Corwin and freedom.
- **Corwin Varrick**, 24. *Voice:* nervous charm, loud laugh at the end of every sentence. *Wants:* Philippa, and his debt gone.
- **Hester Quill**, housekeeper, 66. *Voice:* flat Brannoch accent, never wastes a word, jingles her keys. *Wants:* justice for the Earl. *Secret:* the thirteenth card.
- **Agnes Pike**, maid, 20. *Voice:* quick, giggly, says "begging your pardon" constantly. *Knows:* the corridor sounds.
- **Ambrose Kettle**, footman. *Voice:* shaky, too formal. Found the body at 9:38; touched nothing.
- **Mrs. Ida Bramble**, cook. *Voice:* furious, everything is "my sauce, my swan".
- **Jory Mabb**, Fennick's valet. *Voice:* oily, whispery, hoping for a new master. *Knows:* Fennick wrote to Graye about "the Earl's last night". Will sell this for 5 crowns.
- **Nate Ruddock**, stable boy. *Voice:* broad, cheeky, eats while talking.

## Locations

- **The Corridor.** Agnes's polishing route, the linen closet.
- **The Conservatory.** Locked since Tuesday, cracked roof, frost inside, snow with no footprints.
- **The Billiard Room.** Cues racked, Valden's cigar ash, a game abandoned mid-play.
- **The Chapel.** Small, icy, one candle for the Earl. Nobody prayed there tonight; the candle was never relit and the kneeler is dusty.
- **The Kitchen and Scullery.** Copper pots, sorbet dishes, Pewter the sick cat.
- **The Stable Passage.** Horse smell, lanterns, Nate.
- **Guest Rooms.** Graye's (medical bag), Fennick's (writing case), Philippa's (unwritten journal, no fresh ink, which disproves her "writing").

## Threats

- **Doctor Graye, cornered.** HP 10. Attack +2, scalpel d6. Will surrender when faced by two armed heroes or when Valden tells him to.
- **The Blizzard.** Anyone outside more than 10 minutes rolls Might DC 13 or takes d6 cold damage.
- **The Second Cup.** Poison: d8 damage per 10 minutes until treated (Wits DC 13).
- **Wrongful accusation.** Harrow arrests whoever the heroes name.

## Clocks

- **The Feast Continues:** 1) 10:00 salmon, everyone must be at table; 2) 10:30 game broth, the Countess grows impatient; 3) 11:30 sugared violets, she demands a name; 4) midnight, the thirteenth course is uncovered.
- **The Second Cup:** 1) 10:30 Graye asks the kitchen for chocolate; 2) 10:50 he adds the tincture in the pantry (a hero watching the pantry sees it); 3) 11:00 it reaches Philippa; 4) 11:40 she dies if untreated.
- **Graye's Nerve:** 1) calm; 2) when the snuffbox letter is found, he cleans his spectacles often; 3) when the conservatory is opened, he saddles a horse; 4) he flees into the snow.

## Rewards

- The Countess's gratitude: Lady Ashcombe's townhouse deed, held by Fennick, returned; 200 crowns to each hero.
- Junia may forgive the Countess's mortgage and Corwin's debts.
- Philippa is freed; she and Corwin ask the heroes to stand at their wedding.
- Valden's sword, for the hero who proved his friend's guilt fairly.

## Endings

- **Triumph (The Debt Paid):** Graye is named with proof before midnight, Philippa lives, and the Countess's lie is forgiven. At dawn the snow stops and the Countess sets the thirteenth plate one last time, for the Earl, uncovered.
- **Bittersweet (Late Supper):** Graye is exposed, but Philippa died of the chocolate, or Graye froze on the moors. The Hall feels colder every winter after.
- **Disaster (The Wrong Name):** the heroes accuse the Countess, Valden or Corwin. Graye helps hang them. Months later, Hester Quill is found at the bottom of the cellar stair.

## Off the Map

- **People:** Reverend Silas Orme (village vicar), Widow Petra Haldane (innkeeper at the Crooked Elm), Bartholomew Kell (gamekeeper), Lady Annis Brightwater (gossip), Quentin Slade (solicitor).
- **Places:** Brannoch village, the Crooked Elm inn, Morrow Fell gatehouse, the family crypt under the chapel, the frozen fishpond.
- **If they leave the story:** the snow keeps them in; the clocks run, Philippa drinks the chocolate, and at midnight Hester's card names Graye anyway. He flees into the storm.
